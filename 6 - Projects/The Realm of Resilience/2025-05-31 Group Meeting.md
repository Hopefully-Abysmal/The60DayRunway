Here’s a checklist of key things to keep in mind or prepare for each agenda item in your meeting:

---

### 📝 **1. Memorandums of Understanding (MOUs)**

- **Clarify ownership terms**: Confirm dual-use clauses (e.g. nonprofit gets perpetual rights to use; you retain right to gamify/deploy elsewhere).
    
- **Spell out compensation expectations**: Note $3k/month need vs $1k funding offer, and intention to supplement with external work.
    
- **Define scope explicitly**: Phase 1 (Disaster Relief platform), Phase 2 (Companion app).
    
- **Include fallback clauses**: What happens if funding falls through? Or you can't contribute full-time?
    

---

### 💰 **2. Funding Discussion**

- **Breakdown known funding**: From the nonprofit, Ben Smith (truck driving), Daniel (graphic/design), other sources?
    
- **Estimate total runway needed for Phase 1 + Phase 2**
    
- **Prep pitch or overview for external partners/donors**
    
- **Note time constraints**: Your ability to contribute depends on hitting $3k/month.
    

---

### 🎯 **3. More Grants to Apply For**

- **Have 2–3 promising grant names prepped?** (If not, assign someone to research local/state/tech-for-good grants.)
    
- **Create a Grant Tracker**: Owner, Deadline, Status, Notes
    
- **Assign a “grant writer” lead** or split by areas of expertise
    

---

### 🔄 **4. Phase 1 / Phase 2 Clarification**

- **Phase 1 =** Disaster coordination site, roles, routing, Airtable replacement.
    
- **Phase 2 =** Companion app (location tracking, push notifications, local missions), gamification features (badges, trust roles).
    
- **Timeline & funding milestones**: What’s MVP for each?
    

---

### 🧠 **5. Branding**

- **Revisit name shortlist**: FieldReady, CivicRoot, KindGrid, Flockwork (or generate more).
    
- **Define vibe**: Does it cover both disaster and “sunny sky” scenarios?
    
- **Consider domain availability + app stores**.
    
- **Decide on interim name if rebrand isn’t finalized yet.**
    

---

### 🧪 **6. Surveymonkey**

- **Review question drafts if available** (esp. around branding).
    
- **Decide what to ask the community** vs internal stakeholders.
    
- **Confirm who’s managing the survey** (Transfer to Ben? Set deadline?)
    

---

### 📢 **7. R4 Public Disclosure**

- **What exactly are you disclosing?** (Project goals, MOU terms, team, roadmap?)
    
- **Confirm what’s okay to publish now vs later** (e.g. pre-grant announcements, potential partnerships).
    
- **Format**: Press release? Website post? Public roadmap?
    
- **Message tone**: Transparent, collaborative, hopeful.
    

---
