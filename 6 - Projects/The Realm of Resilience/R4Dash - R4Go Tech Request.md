---

## ✅ **MVP Needs – Build Phase (June–July)**

We're building a lean but powerful prototype. Here's what we're prioritizing now:

---
### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 1. **Volunteer Profiles & Role Customization**

- Help structure lightweight user profiles with **publicly visible badges**, **optional identity validation**, and **self-assigned roles**.
    
- Looking for existing profile systems or models we could adapt.
    
---
### 🗺️ 2. **Map-Based Task Assignments**

- Geo-tagged task system for volunteers to browse, claim, and complete real-world actions.
    
- Looking for input on **route planning**, **on-route task batching**, or **efficient location filters**.
    
---
### 🏅 3. **Initial Gamification Layer**

- Duolingo-style badges tied to real-world tasks (e.g., deliveries, wellness checks, scouting needs).
    
- Seeking collaborators to test early badge logic and explore **what motivates volunteers**.
    
---
### 📬 4. **Needs Reporting + Matching**

- Basic intake form for people to report what they need.
    
- Self-matching system to pair needs with available volunteers (like Uber for mutual aid).
    
- Seeking insights on **sector taxonomy standards** and **intake triage logic**.
    

---

## 🌱 **Future & Parallel Needs – Co-Development Invitations**

We're also looking ahead. If you're working on similar problems, let’s sync up:

---
### 🧠 Trust, Vetting & Microcredentials

- Chain-of-trust model: verified roles based on history, reviews, or in-person ID checks.
    
- “Self-selected vs validated” capabilities.
    
- Seeking partners to prototype trust scores or integrate with tools like **ID.me**, **ebay-style ratings**, or **microcredential ecosystems**.
    
---
### 🌐 Asynchronous + Planetary Participation

- Translation tools, timezone-agnostic coordination, remote role design.
    
- Help us design systems for **info management without central bottlenecks**.
    
- Interested in toolchains you’ve used: Google Sheets? Slack bots? Airtable replacements?
    
---
### 🌍 TAK & Edge-Networking for Offline Mode

- TAK-style visualization: map of “who’s where, doing what.”
    
- Local server discovery, peer sync, offline-first logic.
    
- If you’ve worked on **edge networking** or **austere environment coordination**, we’d love your technical insights.
    
---
### 📊 Open Standards for Needs/People/Resources DB

- Help design or reference a schema to log:
    
    - Needs (indexed + urgent)
        
    - Resources (deployable, available)
        
    - People (with skills, roles, readiness)
        
- Seeking feedback on **where this lives**, and who holds the index.
