### Between Landon Dahle and 4R (Resilience, Relief & Recovery Reach)

## 1. Purpose

This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) outlines the scope, rights, and responsibilities for the collaborative development of the “R4 Buildout” platform — a public-interest tool supporting both disaster logistics coordination and sunny-sky community engagement.

---

## 2. Parties

- **Developer:** Landon Dahle
    
- **Organization:** 4R (Resilience, Relief & Recovery Reach), herein referred to as “4R”
    

---

## 3. Scope of Work

This project is divided into two core development phases:

### Phase 1 — Disaster Relief Platform Update (Current Website)

- Transform the existing WNC-Supply-Sites codebase into a fully-featured disaster relief product
    
- Key deliverables include:
    
    - Volunteer opportunity listing & event coordination system
        
    - Admin/dispatch control panel
        
        - Functional migration off of Airtable as the coordination frontend
            

### Phase 2 — Companion Mobile App _(Deferred)_

This phase will be addressed in a future addendum and includes:

- Geolocation-aware task assignment
    
- Push notifications
    
- Real-world gamified engagement (e.g., quest systems similar to Pokémon Go)
    
- Low-bandwidth, offline-compatible disaster-mode UX

## 4. Intellectual Property & Licensing

All code, assets, and platform components developed by Landon Dahle under this agreement shall be licensed to 4R under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)**. This ensures:

- **Perpetual, unalienable use rights** for 4R
    
- **Freedom to audit, reuse, and adapt** the code for public benefit
    
- **Retention of creator rights**: Landon retains the right to redeploy, adapt, and recontextualize the work for future projects, including but not limited to gamified derivatives and educational platforms.
    

This clause ensures 4R’s access is never revoked, while preserving Landon’s ability to expand the work in aligned or adjacent contexts.

## 5. Compensation

4R has currently allocated **$1,000** for this buildout. Landon Dahle states that sustainable operation of his responsibilities requires a **minimum of $4,000/month** to cover student loan repayments and operating expenses.

As such:

- Landon shall seek supplemental funding (see Section 6)
    
- Time commitment shall be adjusted in proportion to funds received:
    
    - **Full-time availability** if $4,000+/mo is secured
        
    - **Part-time (~10-15 hours/week)** if only partial funding is available
        
    - **Volunteer** wherever possible otherwise.

## 6. Supplemental Contributions & Flexibility

To ensure project continuity:

- **Ben Smith** has expressed willingness to support the buildout via earnings from truck driving and other personal contributions.
    
- **Daniel Lindenberger** is collaborating with Landon on identifying freelance design and co-development opportunities to bridge financial gaps.
    

Should these or other efforts fail to secure necessary income, Landon may need to prioritize paid work elsewhere. Both parties acknowledge this potential constraint and will maintain open communication to adjust scope or timelines as needed.

---

## 7. Collaboration & Communication

All parties agree to:

- Maintain regular updates and coordination via shared project management tools (e.g., Discord, GitHub, Google Docs)
    
- Document all progress transparently and in alignment with the open-source ethos
    
- Prioritize high-resilience, low-friction design suitable for both crisis and non-crisis scenarios
    

---

## 8. Amendments & Exit Conditions

- Either party may request modification of this MOU with two weeks’ written notice.
    
- In the event of major shifts in funding or availability, a revised agreement or development pause may be negotiated.
    
- Termination of this MOU does not revoke any licensing rights already granted to either party.
