# **Historical Eras & Unlocking Metrics**

The idea of guiding the community from early **historical stages** (like cavemen or medieval times) to the **modern era** is powerful. To make this dynamic, communities must meet specific **metrics** that represent the **advancement of society**, which will then **unlock the next era**.

---

### **1. Prehistoric Era (Cavemen)**

#### **Era Goal**: Community Survival

- **Metrics to Unlock the Next Era**:
    
    - **Self-sufficiency**: Achieve **basic food production** (e.g., hunting, gathering, and simple agriculture).
        
    - **Community Cohesion**: Regular **tribal meetings** for decision-making, governance, and basic social structures.
        
    - **Resource Management**: Basic **fire control** and **water access** (community-managed).
        
    - **Tool Usage**: Crafting **stone tools** and **primitive shelters**.
        
- **Incentives**:
    
    - Physical **tools** (e.g., wooden tools, baskets) for completing survival-related tasks.
        
    - **Small-scale community improvements** such as **campfires** or **shelter upgrades**.
        

---

### **2. Ancient Era (Agrarian Societies)**

#### **Era Goal**: Transition from Nomadic to Settled Communities

- **Metrics to Unlock the Next Era**:
    
    - **Food Security**: Achieve **basic agricultural production** (e.g., farming and animal husbandry).
        
    - **Infrastructure Development**: Build **early homes**, **storage systems**, and **irrigation**.
        
    - **Social Structures**: Form **leadership systems** and basic **governance** (e.g., councils).
        
    - **Trade & Economy**: Introduce **barter systems** and local **trade routes**.
        
- **Incentives**:
    
    - **Access to new technologies** like **plows**, **seeds**, or **grain storage**.
        
    - **Agricultural tools** and access to **local markets**.
        

---

### **3. Feudal Era (Medieval Times)**

#### **Era Goal**: Establishment of Stable Societies

- **Metrics to Unlock the Next Era**:
    
    - **Sustainable Agriculture**: Implement **crop rotation** and **diversified farming** techniques.
        
    - **Infrastructure**: Establish **fortifications** (e.g., walls, castles) and **roads**.
        
    - **Education & Religion**: **Establish schools**, religious institutions, and early forms of **literacy**.
        
    - **Social Stability**: Develop systems for **law** and **order** (e.g., local justice, trade laws).
        
- **Incentives**:
    
    - **Craftsmanship** and **guilds** for enhanced resource gathering.
        
    - **Trading posts** for exchange of goods between communities.
        
    - **Educational benefits** for improved skills or trade knowledge.
        

---

### **4. Industrial Revolution**

#### **Era Goal**: Mass Production & Urbanization

- **Metrics to Unlock the Next Era**:
    
    - **Energy Production**: Develop **alternative energy sources** (e.g., early coal power, windmills).
        
    - **Industry and Manufacturing**: Establish **factories** and **workshops**.
        
    - **Public Health**: Introduce **public health** and **hygiene** systems (e.g., sanitation, hospitals).
        
    - **Education**: Expand **formal education** and **literacy** to the masses.
        
- **Incentives**:
    
    - **Access to industrial tools** and **production methods**.
        
    - **Urbanization rewards** (e.g., access to **housing** or **factories**).
        
    - **Technology-based quests** (e.g., **steam engine creation** or **railroad building**).
        

---

### **5. Information Age (Modern Era)**

#### **Era Goal**: Digital Revolution and Globalization

- **Metrics to Unlock the Next Era**:
    
    - **Technology Infrastructure**: Develop **internet connectivity**, **data centers**, and **cloud computing**.
        
    - **Education & Innovation**: Achieve widespread **technological literacy** (e.g., coding, digital literacy).
        
    - **Global Connectivity**: Promote **global trade**, **interconnectivity**, and **cultural exchange**.
        
    - **Environmental Sustainability**: Develop **green tech** (e.g., solar, wind, sustainable farming).
        
- **Incentives**:
    
    - **Access to new technologies** like **smart devices**, **renewable energy solutions**, and **space exploration**.
        
    - **Access to global markets** for trade and **investment opportunities**.
        

---

### **6. Future Era (Sustainable Society and Space Exploration)**

#### **Era Goal**: Advancing Beyond Earth

- **Metrics to Unlock**:
    
    - **Sustainability**: Achieve **zero waste** communities, **energy independence**, and **circular economies**.
        
    - **Space Exploration**: **Space colonies**, **advanced interplanetary travel**, and **terraforming** projects.
        
    - **Universal Basic Needs**: Achieve **global equity** in access to **education**, **healthcare**, and **basic necessities**.
        
- **Incentives**:
    
    - **Access to cutting-edge technologies** (e.g., **nanotechnology**, **fusion energy**).
        
    - **Space missions** and **interplanetary trade** as community-wide goals.
        

---

## **Community Cartographer**

The **Community Cartographer** would serve as the **central visual tool** where communities can track their **progression** through these **eras**. It would display:

- **Era advancement**: Communities’ advancement through time (linked to real-world actions).
    
- **Engagement levels**: Public metrics visible to all members.
    
- **Quest completion**: Progress through quests, linked to **community achievements**.
    

### **Cartography Display Options**

- **Regional Evolution**: Each district can show the **historical timeline** of that region and its **current status**. Only visible at community hubs or to **community leaders**.
    
- **Artificial Scarcity**: **Only available at central hubs** or to community leaders to **promote interaction** and **physical engagement**.
    

---

### **Progression Timeline**

The **era progression model** is a way to gamify **community development** and **real-world participation**, with **metrics tied to tangible, actionable goals**:

- **Short-term metrics** drive immediate engagement.
    
- **Long-term metrics** push communities toward more **structural, systemic improvements**.
    

This model creates an **interactive, evolving timeline** where players are incentivized to progress from **primitive societies** toward **space-faring civilizations**, while addressing **real-world issues** like **sustainability**, **collaboration**, and **economic equity**.
