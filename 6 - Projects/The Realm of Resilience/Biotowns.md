## **BioTowns**
**Community Engagement Through Action**

---

A lightweight, badge-based platform for coordinating community engagement and disaster response. Designed to incentivize real-world action through visible progress and peer accountability, building on the existing (and to be developed) **WNC Disaster Relief** framework.

---

### **Purpose**

To increase local engagement, volunteering, and coordinated response to community needs using a gamified system of visible progress, public accountability, and social motivation.

---

### **Core Features**

**Badge-Based Incentives**

- Users earn badges for verified actions (e.g., volunteering, attending events, hosting efforts).
	
- Badges are public and act as social signals, promoting peer motivation (similar to Duolingo streaks).

---

**Action Tracking & Verification**

- Tasks like cleanup, food distribution, and wellness checks are logged and reviewed.
	
- Verified actions increase a user’s trust score and unlock more responsibilities or perks.

---

**Needs Assessment Integration**
    
- Communities can report needs directly through the platform.
	
- Volunteers match themselves to tasks based on urgency, location, and skill tags.

---

**Role Customization**
    
- Users can take on roles such as “Coordinator,” “Scout,” “Medic,” or “Communicator,” with associated responsibilities and unlockable tools.

---

**Map-Based Task Interface**
    
- Users view a local map showing task density and badge hotspots.

- Tasks “unlock” as engagement in an area increases.


---

**Public Leaderboards & Community Stats**
    
- Highlight top volunteers, active zones, and areas in need.
	
- Encourages friendly competition and mutual support.

---

**Community Organizer Dashboard:**
Dashboard of MetaCrisis Doughnut Charts (see Daniels work) on community, state, and national levels to foster **Intra-community Collaboration**. (all inter-accessible by community organizers for decision-making)

---

**Ideally this would enable:**
- **Support lower-scoring communities** to promote **community solidarity** and **growth**.
- **Cross-community challenges** to encourage **mutual aid** and **collaboration**.

---

**Low-Tech Compatibility**

Designed for use during disasters: mobile-first, low-bandwidth, offline caching.
- Dedicated diseaster mode, if location is detected to be within diseaster region all gamification automatically turned off (ofc save metrics as possible so we can recognize the real heros though)

---

### **Target Use Cases**

- Disaster relief coordination (post-storm, flood, wildfire, etc.).
    
- Ongoing community maintenance (trash pickup, tool sharing, neighbor wellness).
    
- Civic engagement (town halls, census drives, local surveys).
    

---

### **Deployment**

- Mobile app for volunteers, webapp for organizers (also mobile app but like, they need the bigger picture)
- Build on the backend of the **WNC Disaster Relief** framework.
- Management portals hosted on **disastersupplysites.org** with federated community subdomains (e.g., townname.disastersupplysites.org).

---

### **Long-Term Vision**

- Establish a decentralized network of **BioTowns**, each with its own badge ecosystem and local culture.
    
- Use behavioral nudges and social feedback loops to normalize community care.
    
- Layer in additional incentives (e.g., local discounts, certifications) over time.
    

---

### **Next Steps**

1. **Build MVP** focused on badge system, needs matching, and basic mapping.
    
2. **Pilot with WNC partners** and existing disaster relief networks.
    
3. **Scale by onboarding local hubs** (libraries, shelters, community centers).
