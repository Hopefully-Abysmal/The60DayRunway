### ~**An Introduction**~

**What is The Society of Seekers?**
- A platform combining **geocaching**, **storytelling**, and **community engagement** through **real-world exploration**.
- Aimed at fostering **community development** through **physical and digital interactions**.


---

### ~**Core Concept**~
**Goal**: Incentivize real-world action while building social cohesion and community growth through ***exploration***.  
~
**Finite Games**: Structured tasks with specific goals.
**Infinite Games**: Ongoing engagement, focused on ***long-term growth and participation***. [1]

### ~**Exploration Gamification**~
**Public Metrics for Engagement**:    
- Use **percentage-based metrics** (e.g., map unlocked, community explored) instead of traditional points. 
- Display these metrics **publicly** to promote **social pressure** and **community participation**.

---

**Immersive Activities**:
- Partner with **Garmin** and/or shoot for Android Auto / Apple Carplay integrations for **GPS** immersion, linking quests to real-world exploration.
    - Use **OpenStreetMap** and **historical layers** to provide **contextual exploration**.
- **Geocaching Integration**:
	**NFC coins and QR codes** allow for **scavenger hunts** and **real-world treasure hunts**.

### ~**The Community Cartographer**~
Physical Engagement and Community Events

![[Pasted image 20250528184803.png]]

---

Dashboard of Doughnut Charts (see Daniels work) to foster **Intra-community Collaboration**:
- **Support lower-scoring communities** to promote **community solidarity** and **growth**.
- **Cross-community challenges** to encourage **mutual aid** and **collaboration**.

---

Non-linearized **Historical Era Progression**:
- **District Charicatures**: **Geographical and cultural regions** with **customizable aesthetics** based on historical and cultural themes.
- Communities start in **early stages** (e.g., cavemen, medieval) and unlock the **modern era** through real-world actions (e.g., energy self-sufficiency, community collaboration).

---

**Turnstile/Portal Mechanism**:
- Physical and digital **portals** that allow communities to **travel through time** by completing **tasks** or **missions**.

### **Community Events**

- **CITO (Cache in Trash Out)**:
    
    - Community-driven efforts to **clean up local areas**, with **rewards for participants**.
        
        
- **Capture the Flag (CTF)**:
    
    - **Community security tests**, where communities compete to prove **resilience** (e.g., ethical hacking, community pen tests).
