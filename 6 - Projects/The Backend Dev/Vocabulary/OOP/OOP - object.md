### ✅ **Use `[[OOP - object]]` when:**

You're discussing objects as **instances of classes**, especially in:

- Object-Oriented Programming theory (encapsulation, methods, state)
    
- Class-instance relationships
    
- Design patterns and behavioral modeling
    
- Class-based languages like Java, C++, or Ruby
    

📎 _Think: “What is this object’s role in the system? What behavior or identity does it model?”_
