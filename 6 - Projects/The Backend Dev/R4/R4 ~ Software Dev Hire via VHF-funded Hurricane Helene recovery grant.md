## 🛠️ **Junior Software Developer (Full-Time, Remote – NC Only)**

**$35/hr | 6-month contract (potential renewal) | Disaster Resilience Tech**

**🗺️ Who We Are**  
We’re the team behind R4 and WNC Supply Sites—an open-source, community-driven platform helping coordinate disaster relief and supply chain logistics across Western North Carolina. We’re expanding to serve a national audience via [disastersupplysites.org](http://disastersupplysites.org/), and we’re hiring full-time developers to help make that happen.

This role is made possible by a **VHF-funded Hurricane Helene recovery grant**, so eligible candidates **must be based in North Carolina** and have **lost employment as a result of Helene**.

---

## 🎯 **Role Overview**

We’re looking for a **self-directed, early-career software developer** to join our mission-driven team and contribute to real-world disaster relief software. You’ll collaborate with experienced developers, community organizers, and volunteer coordinators to build tools that directly support resilience efforts across multiple counties.

---

## 🔧 **What You’ll Do**

- Implement backend and frontend features across two live codebases:
    
    - `wnc-supply-sites`: Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL (JDBI), Mustache templates
        
    - `r4dash-frontend-mvp`: React, TypeScript, Supabase, Tailwind, Mapbox
        
- Translate user needs into usable code, often working async with community partners
    
- Help replace our Airtable-based workflows with open-source equivalents
    
- Enter location datasets and ensure API integrations (Twilio, Mapbox, etc.) work smoothly
    
- Handle user data responsibly and support secure system design
    

---

- Live in North Carolina and were displaced professionally due to Hurricane Helene
    
- Are early in your software career and eager to learn fast and build real tools
    
- Have _some_ experience (or strong curiosity to learn) with:
    
    - **Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL**
        
    - **React, TypeScript, Supabase**

- Are motivated, communicative, and can work independently in a startup-style environment
    
- Want to work on tech that makes a meaningful impact in the real world
