**CBET Crash Prep Plan (July 2025 Target)**

---

### 🔄 Goal:

Pass the **Certified Biomedical Equipment Technician (CBET)** exam by **August 1, 2025**, using accelerated, targeted study strategies leveraging existing Biomedical Engineering knowledge.

---

### 📊 Timeline Breakdown (4 Weeks)

#### Week 1 (June 17–23): Foundation & Survey

- ✅ Review CBET candidate handbook & exam outline
    
- ✅ Skim ANSI/AAMI standards mentioned in CBET study guides
    
- ✅ Review anatomy/physiology relevant to medical equipment
    
- ✅ Download flashcard decks (Quizlet, Brainscape, Anki)
    
- ✅ Register for exam (if not already done)
    

#### Week 2 (June 24–30): Devices & Tech Systems

- ⚖ Focus Areas: Diagnostic imaging (X-ray, MRI, Ultrasound), life support, monitoring
    
- ⚖ Learn failure modes, calibration & PM procedures
    
- ⚖ Review safety standards (NFPA 99, electrical leakage, grounding)
    
- ✅ Daily timed quizzes (one section/day)
    

#### Week 3 (July 1–7): Troubleshooting & Management

- 🧹 Practice interpreting maintenance logs, documentation formats
    
- 🤐 Learn AEM strategies, risk-based prioritization
    
- 🪡 Review equipment lifecycle, service contract management
    
- 🤔 Study IT integration (EMR, PACS, HL7 basics)
    
- ✅ Take full-length practice test (~175 Qs)
    

#### Week 4 (July 8–14): High-Yield Review

- 📊 Fill knowledge gaps based on practice test results
    
- 📅 Schedule light mock exam every 2 days
    
- 📖 Make one-page cheat sheets per topic
    
- ☑️ Revisit high-yield flashcards daily
