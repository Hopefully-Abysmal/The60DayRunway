## 🌐 Neurosecurity & Neuroethics

### 💡 Definition & Scope

- **Neurosecurity** applies cybersecurity principles to protect neural devices and data: ensuring confidentiality, integrity, availability of neural interfaces, safeguarding mental autonomy and free will ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neurosecurity?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Neurosecurity")).
    
- **Neuroethics** examines the societal, legal, and moral dimensions of neurotechnology—rights like mental privacy, integrity, cognitive liberty, and fair use ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06281?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Minding rights: Mapping ethical and legal foundations of 'neurorights'")).
    

---

### 🔍 Emerging Trends

- **Neurorights movement**: Global momentum for legal frameworks around mental privacy and freedom (e.g., UNESCO, Council of Europe) ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06281?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Minding rights: Mapping ethical and legal foundations of 'neurorights'")).
    
- **Privacy-preserving BCIs**: Growing research on built-in privacy/security by design frameworks for devices like EEG-based BCIs ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.11394?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Privacy-Preserving Brain-Computer Interfaces: A Systematic Review")).
    
- **Ethical design integration**: Embedding ethics into neurotech R&D—from ideation to product rollout ([sciencedirect.com](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0896627322004135?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Building a culture of responsible neurotech: Neuroethics as socio ...")).
    
- **Cyber–neuroscience convergence**: Merging cybersecurity and neuroscience to counteract brain-targeted attacks and manipulation ([qmenta.com](https://www.qmenta.com/blog/top-5-trends-in-neuroscience-you-need-to-know-in-2025?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Top 5 Trends in Neuroscience You Need to Know in 2025 - qmenta")).
