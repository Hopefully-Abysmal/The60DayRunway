> There’s much more beneath the surface 🎯 Neurotech spans far beyond the fields we covered—it's a broad and rapidly evolving domain. Here’s an expanded map of the major fields:

---

## 🧠 Expanded Fields of [[Neurotechnology]]

1. **[[Cognitive Enhancement & Assessment]]**
    
    - Technologies for improving or measuring cognition (e.g., cognition‑boosting apps, adaptive testing). [[10 major types of Neurotech]]
        
2. **[[Neuropharmacology]]**
    
    - Smart drugs, novel psychoactive treatments, and drug delivery systems targeting the nervous system. [[10 major types of Neurotech]]
        
3. **[[Neuromonitoring _ Imaging]]**
    
    - From single-neuron electrophysiology to fMRI, PET, EEG, fNIRS, ultrasound-based brain tracking. [[The Future of Neurotechnology - Forbes]]
        
4. **[[Neurofeedback]]**
    
    - Real-time brainwave visualization and training (EEG-/fMRI-based) for self-optimization. [[How Neurotherapy Works]]
        
5. **[[Neuromodulation (non-pharma)]]**
    
    - Electric/Magnetic/Ultrasound stimulation: TMS, tDCS/tACS, focused ultrasound, ECT, peripheral/nerve stimulation. [[Neurotech Classification Systems]]
        
6. **[[Invasive Neuromodulation & Neural Implants]]**
    
    - Deep brain stimulation, dorsal root/spinal/gastric/cranial stimulators, brain–computer implants. [[Literature Review: Neurotechnology - Nuffield]], [[Neurotech Classification Systems]]
        
7. **[[Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs)]]**
    
    - Includes invasive, minimally invasive (stent-based), and non-invasive BCIs for communication and control.
        
8. **[[Neural Prosthetics & Neurorehabilitation]]**
    
    - Brain-controlled prosthetics, spinal rehabilitation, VR‑robotics‑driven recovery platforms. [[Neurotech Directory]]
        
9. **[[Neural Engineering & Neurosystems Design]]**
    
    - Neural circuit modeling, neuromechanics, neural tissue engineering, network neuroscience. [[Neural engineering]], [[Emerging Frontiers of Neuroengineering]]
        
10. **[[Computational & Theoretical Neuroscience]]**
    
    - Mathematical and computational modeling, neuromorphic computing, computational psychiatry. [[Computational neuroscience]]
        
11. **[[Neural Tissue Regeneration]]**
    
    - Stem-cell scaffolds, biomaterials for regrowing neural tissue and repairing spinal/nerve injuries. [[Neural engineering]]
        
12. **[[Neurorobotics]]**
    
    - Robotics guided by neural circuits or brain inspiration; robot‑brain co‑simulation platforms.
        
13. **[[Neural Enhancement _ Augmentation]]**
    
    - Enhancement beyond therapy—memory stimulation, mood control, personality modulation. [[Neural engineering]]
        
14. **[[Cyberware _ Embodied Neural Implants]]**
    
    - Sci-fi-style semi-permanent implants for sensory or computational augmentation.
        
15. **[[Neurohydrodynamics & Biofluidics]]**
    
    - Study and engineering of brain fluid environments: CSF dynamics, implantable pumps, shunts. [[Neurohydrodynamics]]
        
16. **[[Neurosecurity & Neuroethics]]**
    
    - Security for brain devices (think “anti-malware for the mind”), and ethics/privacy of neural data.

Many overlaps exist (e.g., closed-loop BCIs combine modulation, monitoring, and computation).
