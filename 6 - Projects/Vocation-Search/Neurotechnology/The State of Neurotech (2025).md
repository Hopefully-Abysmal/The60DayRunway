## 🧩 How to Use These Resources

1. **Compile Base Company List**
    
    - Extract company names, descriptions, URLs from Holland, F6S, Dealroom, VentureRadar.
        
    - Merge overlapping entries and categorize by field (BCI, implants, wearables, neuro‑security, etc.).
        
2. **Enrich with GitHub Data**
    
    - Search within “awesome‑bci”, NDK, and Berkeley repos for affiliated startups.
        
    - Add relevant OSS-based or community-oriented companies (e.g. OpenBCI).
        
3. **Validate & Update Details**
    
    - Visit each company’s website and LinkedIn to capture tech scope, funding stage, location.
        
    - Add notes about ethical/security awareness where available (e.g., privacy position, neurorights alignment).
        
4. **Map to Ecosystem Visual**
    
    - Organize companies into a clean brain-shaped mindmap or ecosystem graph.
        
    - Color-code by field and annotate if they have explicit ethics/privacy policies or practices.
        
5. **Source Citations**
    
    - Use the sources above as citations for your write-up and ecosystem visuals.
