# Sub-domains
### 🜁 Exploratory & Signature

- **/rituals** – Daily or weekly creative practices; make it mystical (Creostic "prayers" or prompts).
    
- **/vault** – A “Babel Ideas Archive”–like gallery of unfinished thoughts, half-baked inventions, dreams, or tools.
    
- **/codex** – Interactive knowledge map of your philosophies (can be visual or just deeply linked markdown pages).
    
- **/mirror** – An experimental identity test or question flow that “reveals” an aspect of the visitor (sort of like an alignment chart).
    
- **/gallery** – Display your art, icons, assets, or even memes tied to your ideas.
    

### 🜂 Playful & SEO-Boosting

- **/glossary** – Define your personal lexicon. (E.g., what is "Creostic"? "Interphrase"? "The 60-Day Runway"?) Great for SEO.
    
- **/library** – Curated reading list (books, essays, tools, videos) with annotations—supports search and demonstrates thought leadership.
    
- **/timeline** – Chronological or mythological unfolding of your projects (“The Birth of Aeon,” “The Founding of ARX”).
    
- **/summon** – Fun contact page styled as a spell summoning ritual.
    

### 🜃 Storefront Enhancements

- **/foundry** – Frame your shop as a mythical forge of artifacts. Custom descriptions help SEO (e.g., "summoning sigil stickers").
    
- **/offerings** – Let people commission you like consulting services, art, writing, or tech help—ritualistically named tiers.
    
- **/guild** – “Join the guild” subscription tier or patronage for ongoing content, early access, or secret lore.

## 💡 Technical Constraints & Build Strategy

### Lovable.Dev Build Targets (This Weekend)

- `/` (Landing page or “The Oasis”): Iconic map-style portal (possibly animated or interactive)
