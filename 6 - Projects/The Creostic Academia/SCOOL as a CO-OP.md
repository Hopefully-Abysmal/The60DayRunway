### 🧠 **What’s a Co-op (in this context)?**

A **cooperative** is a legal structure and governance model where **members collectively own and control** the organization. In a **platform co-op**, users or contributors share ownership, decision-making power, and profits (if any).

---

### ✅ **Why SCOOL as a Co-op Makes Sense**

|**Co-op Element**|**SCOOL Fit**|

|**Collective ownership**|Learners, educators, contributors, and curators can all hold fractional stakes|
