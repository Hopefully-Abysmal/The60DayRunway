Howdy! 
My name is Landon Dahle, and I am the founder of The Augment Realities Experience (ARX) Foundation. My cofounder Adam Miri and I are working on setting up an open education framework we are calling the Scalable Community for Open Online Learning (SCOOL, not to be confused with SKOOL); a proposal for a decentralized but structured creative community and open education platform to ensure that knowledge is open to all.
We are currently building out an MVP of one such education platform that could exist on the SCOOL framework we are internally framing as "The ARX Portal", a learning management system based on the concept of individual's personal knowledge management systems and the automation of tracking of the content consumed by the user at its core.
